## how it works

01

link your accounts

spotify, last.fm, apple music, whatever. we just read your history. we don't post anything.

02

see your taste overlap

we compare your top artists, genres, and that one album you listened to 40 times in a week.

03

add people

send a request. if they accept, you can see what they're playing. that's it. no chat, no feeds, no noise.

## why this exists

every music app tries to recommend you stuff. spotify gives you the same playlist as everyone else. last.fm killed their friends page. discord has no way to find people by taste.

i just want to know: who else is in the 0.05% of listeners for have a nice life? who else has loveless in their top 10 every single year? this site answers that.

## what you get

  • profile — your top artists, recent tracks, playlists. customize the colors if you want.
  • discover — find people by genre, artist, or just scroll and see who's online.
  • friends — see what your people are playing right now. no stories, no likes, just music.
  • stats — how many hours this month, your genre breakdown, your obscure taste score.
