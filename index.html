## how it works

01

make an account

name + password. no email. no verification. 10 seconds.

02

link your accounts

spotify, last.fm, whatever. we just read your history.

03

find your people

see who shares your taste. add them. see what they're playing.

## what you get

  • profile — your top artists, recent tracks, playlists. customize colors.
  • discover — find people by genre or scroll and see who's online.
  • friends — see what your people are playing. no likes, no feeds, just music.
  • stats — hours, genre breakdown, obscure taste score.
